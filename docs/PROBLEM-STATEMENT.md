# Problem Statement — NestNear

University students who relocate to pursue higher education often struggle to find suitable Paying Guest (PG) accommodation near their campus. Details about rent, deposits, food, facilities, house rules and distance are scattered across brokers, social media posts and individual listings. Students may encounter incomplete or outdated information, unclear additional charges, and limited access to trustworthy resident feedback. Comparing several properties therefore becomes time-consuming and makes it difficult to make an informed accommodation decision.

NestNear proposes a centralized PG discovery platform where students can search by university or locality, filter options by budget and preferences, review property details and resident feedback, save favourites, and compare shortlisted stays. PG owners can maintain basic listing information through a simple owner dashboard.

## Target users
- University students relocating to a new city or changing accommodation.
- PG owners and accommodation managers who want to publish listing details.

## Project objective
Make the PG search process more organized and transparent by presenting key accommodation information in a consistent, comparable format.
