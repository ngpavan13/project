# Product Scope

## MVP inclusions
- Student-facing home page and PG search results.
- Filters for rent, gender/accommodation preference and food inclusion.
- PG detail pages with rent, deposit, recurring charges, amenities, rules and sample reviews.
- Saved PG shortlist and comparison of up to three properties.
- Demo owner login and owner listing form.
- Browser-based persistence for saved, compare and owner-created data.

## Out of scope for this prototype
- Real authentication or authorization.
- Online booking, deposits or payment processing.
- Live availability, maps, route calculations or distance verification.
- Government/identity/property verification.
- Real-time chat, SMS, email or push notifications.
- Production review moderation and dispute resolution.

## Success criteria
1. A student can find listings using search and filters.
2. A student can inspect a listing and understand its stated cost components.
3. A student can save and compare multiple PGs.
4. An owner can add a sample listing and see it in the dashboard.
5. Core pages remain usable on mobile and desktop.
