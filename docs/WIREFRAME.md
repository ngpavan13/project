# NestNear — Text Wireframe

## 1. Home (`/`)
- Header: NestNear brand, Explore PGs, Saved, Compare, For PG owners.
- Hero: value proposition and Explore PGs action.
- Quick search: university/area and monthly budget.
- Featured PG cards.
- Benefits: location, transparent cost details, resident feedback.
- Footer navigation.

## 2. PG search (`/pgs`)
- Page heading and supporting description.
- Filter panel: keyword, maximum rent, accommodation preference, food included.
- Results count and sort control.
- Listing cards with tag, area, sharing type, rating, amenities, rent and deposit.

## 3. PG details (`/pgs/[id]`)
- Property visual, location, audience, room type, rating and rent.
- Enquire, save and compare actions.
- About, facilities, house rules and resident reviews.
- Cost breakdown: rent, food, electricity, maintenance and security deposit.

## 4. Saved (`/saved`)
- Saved PG cards or an empty state with a link to browse.

## 5. Compare (`/compare`)
- Side-by-side table for rent, deposit, area, university distance, room type, food, rating and facilities.

## 6. Owner (`/owner-login`, `/owner-dashboard`)
- Demo sign-in form and credentials.
- Dashboard summary, add-listing form and current demo listings.
